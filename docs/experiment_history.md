# Experiment history

This chronology summarizes the scientific evolution represented by the
repository. Dated configurations and manifests, not this narrative, are the
authority for exact parameters.

1. Random-regular baselines established density, unfolding, spacing, adjacent
   gap-ratio, and IPR conventions.
2. Erdős–Rényi and Chung–Lu studies added component-aware spectra, empirical
   density/CDF unfolding, power-law weight controls, resonance/atom analysis,
   sparse extreme eigenpairs, and degree/spectral-tail matching.
3. Exact labeled-degree rewiring isolated effects not explained by the
   realized degree sequence while preserving simplicity.
4. Preferential-attachment work expanded from BA pilots to affine-linear and
   nonlinear attachment, rewired controls, full-spectrum scans, mobility-edge
   summaries, extreme-edge audits, and three fixed `N=5000` graph microscopes.
5. The clean affine-PA two-part study measured full-spectrum localization and
   spacing, then tested graph-ball/local-mode explanations of fixed adjacent
   non-Perron pairs. It retained failed or unresolved reference descriptions
   rather than turning them into positive evidence.
6. Geometry, tunneling, zero-subspace, and Schur diagnostics were developed as
   controlled corrections and extensions. A separate critical Erdős–Rényi
   benchmark and a random 3-regular Anderson benchmark kept literature models
   distinct from PA.
7. The paired diagonal-disorder study reused exact clean affine-PA graphs and
   evaluated `H(a)=A+a diag(xi)` over eight raw amplitudes with one fixed field
   per graph. The completed report emphasizes finite-size descriptive evidence,
   not a universal critical amplitude.

The final publication combines these paths in one repository while preserving
historical reports and explicit limitations. It does not introduce an
unrequested Watts–Strogatz ensemble to fill the historical “small world” name.
